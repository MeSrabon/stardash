Features

What the Starlink app doesn't show you.

StarDash talks to the same local API as the official app and keeps a history the dish forgets.

Speed and latency

Throughput, latency, packet loss and power over time, and how latency is spread for the dish and the router.

Obstruction map

As a flat chart, a horizon view and a 3D dome, with a time-lapse of how it filled in and a one-click reset.

Satellites overhead

Every Starlink satellite in view, computed from public orbit data, and the one your dish is most likely using.

Outage history

Every drop and alert with its cause, kept between launches, plus public IP changes.

Network devices

Everything on your Wi-Fi with its vendor, signal and traffic, plus mesh nodes and band switches.

Dish controls

Snow melt, sleep schedule, update time, stow, reboot and factory reset, each behind a confirm.

Screens

A full window when you want to dig in.

Local first

Your data stays on your Mac.

StarDash reads the dish at 192.168.100.1 and the router at 192.168.1.1 over your own network. No servers, no analytics, no sign-up.

Beyond your network it only downloads public data: satellite orbits from CelesTrak and the device vendor list from the IEEE. Speed tests run against Cloudflare, and only when you start one.

Optional

Sign in for more.

Signing in to your Starlink account adds billing usage, router settings (Wi-Fi name, password and DNS) and network rules for data limits, online hours and pause timers.

The session is kept in your keychain, and StarDash only contacts starlink.com for these features. Sign out and it's gone.

Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. DownloadGet the latest StarDash.dmg.
  2. Drag to ApplicationsOpen the disk image and drag StarDash into Applications.
  3. Open itOn the same network as your dish. It finds the dish and router by itself.

Builds aren't notarized yet. If macOS says it can't verify the developer, open System Settings › Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/StarDash.app.
